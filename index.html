Research

I study hidden mechanisms and intermediate representations in LLMs, build agentic workflows for real-world operations (e.g., SRE/CISO tasks), and design evaluation frameworks for safety and robustness. I enjoy combining theory (game-theoretic and statistical perspectives) with large-scale empirical studies.
